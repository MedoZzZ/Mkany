# Legal Composition (AdminLTE)

## The Digital Dossier
The Legal application is focused on dense case management, strict deadlines, and interrelated documentation.

## Composition Rules
- **The Heavy Sidebar**: The Legal app is the ONLY application that renders a persistent, deep-charcoal (`#222D32`) left sidebar (`240px` wide). This sidebar handles internal navigation (Clients, Cases, Hearings, Deadlines).
- **Main Content Well**: The area to the right of the sidebar uses a cool gray background (`#ECF0F5`).
- **The Boxed Panel**: Content is divided into rigid `<Panel>` components. Panels are white (`#FFFFFF`), have a `1px` solid border, a `3px` border radius, and a thicker `3px` colored top-border to indicate semantic meaning.
- **Breadcrumbs**: Every page must have a breadcrumb trail to ground the user in the deep hierarchy.
- **Density**: Do not use whitespace generously. Tables must pack as many rows as possible into the viewport. Use strict vertical and horizontal grid lines for tables.
